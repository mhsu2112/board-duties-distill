Cir. 45/ 16.20.00/ 2003-04 dated April 15, 2004 wherein Urban Co-operative Banks were advised that they can invest in bonds / equity of All India Financial Institutions, subject to the limits prescribed in para 8 (ii) and (iii) thereof. 2.
