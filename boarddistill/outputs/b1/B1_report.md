## Methodology & caveats

- **Allowlist only** (primary sources); secondary sources are never cited as evidence.
- **support_signal** is a keyword heuristic (does the provision text name the stated board actor) surfaced for human F2 confirmation — it is not a machine legal claim.
- **U.S. Code** quotes are section-level: the govinfo HTML granule is not paragraph-addressable, so a `(b)` sub-cite pins the whole section (§6.4).
- **SR letters**: numbered attributes live in separate attachments; the cover-letter text (after `SUBJECT:`) is pinned, not the attribute-specific body (§6.4).
- **Guidance-document citations** (SR-letter attachments, exam-manual components, local line numbers) are `unverified` by construction — they cite guidance locations, not addressable primary law. This is itself a sludge signal.
