# Proposed Consolidated GSIB Board Duty Set — v0.1 (illustrative draft)

**What this is:** the target-state board duty list implied by applying every *advanced* reform lever (B0 D1–D9 + B5 levers, as disposed by M. Hsu on July 2, 2026) to the 227-row inventory. Synthesized from `boarddistill/outputs/b5/corpus/master_crosswalk.md`, the lever decompositions, and the statutory floor.

**What it is not:** operative or proposed regulatory text (PRD NG2). Every entry is a `draft` finding downstream of `needs_review` dispositions; several levers require joint rulemaking or statutes that do not yet exist. Quantitative dial values (thresholds, cadences chosen) are deliberately unset. Treat as the working "after" picture for the reform roadmap — the thing a drafting team would start from.

**The headline arithmetic:** ~227 overlapping duty statements across five agencies and five instrument strata → **6 General-Part mechanics + 24 domain duties ≈ 30 consolidated duty statements**, with every statutory-floor obligation intact and every "parallel-but-justified" interest preserved by relation rather than deleted.

---

## Part I — The General Part (stated once, cross-referenced everywhere)

The single largest sludge pattern in the corpus was the same meta-duty restated per domain per agency. These six mechanics replace those restatements; domain duties below state only their *object* and any stricter parameter.

| # | General mechanic | Replaces (pattern) | Source levers |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Framework approval.** For each enumerated framework/policy, the board (or its designated committee) approves initially and reviews on the stated cadence (default annual); the strictest applicable domain cadence prevails. | "board approves a written X framework and reviews it annually" ×~21 clusters | D9 (`ELEVATE_GENERAL_RULE`) |
| G2 | **Reporting.** The board receives periodic management reporting per framework, sufficient in scope and detail; below-threshold matters flow as aggregate reports. | scattered "reports shall go to the board" provisions | D9, D5, R6 |
| G3 | **Delegation & reliance.** Committee execution is permitted where composition rules are met; an IDI duty is deemed satisfied by the parent enterprise framework where risk profiles are substantially the same, documented, with an IDI addendum for bank-specific content. The bank board's independent legal responsibility survives. | the pervasive parallel BHC/IDI approve-oversee-report pairs | D1, D8 (`DEEM_SATISFIED` schema) |
| G4 | **Records.** One documentation standard for board/committee minutes, materials, votes, and capital/planning process records. | BCG-12, CAP-07 and restatements | D9 |
| G5 | **Effectiveness.** One annual board self-assessment satisfying both App. D III.F and SR 21-3. | duplicate assessments | D7 |
| G6 | **Canonical definitions.** One definition each, cross-referenced by all instruments: *independent director* (reconciled test), *risk appetite*, *critical activity* (quantitative; value TBD), *material activities*. | four+ divergent independence tests; undefined supervisory terms | D3, R5, R6, DEFINE_TERM worklist |

## Part II — Consolidated domain duties (the ~24)

Floor = statutory-floor obligation (cannot be deleted, only satisfied). Force = intended stratum of the surviving statement. Every entry lists its source lever; source duty rows trace via the master crosswalk.

**Strategy & risk governance**
1. Approve the **enterprise strategy and risk-governance framework, including the risk-appetite statement** — one document, all risk dimensions (liquidity tolerance and operational-disruption tolerance as distinct modules); IDI addendum via G3. *(Force: regulation + guideline cross-ref · D1, R5, lever 1.13)*
2. Maintain a **risk committee** meeting the consolidated standard (charter, independent chair, quarterly CRO reporting); streamlined IDI certification. *(Floor: 12 U.S.C. 5365(h) · Force: regulation; App. D cross-references · D2)*
3. Approve the **appointment, removal, and compensation of the CRO** (and receive the CRE-independence attestation — OCC-specific control retained). *(Force: regulation, Reg YY home; OCC incorporates by reference · R1)*

**Audit & financial reporting**
4. Maintain **one audit committee** whose composition satisfies FDICIA/Part 363, SOX 301, and exchange standards under the single independence definition (G6). *(Floors: SOX 301; FDI Act §36 · D3)*
5. Through the audit committee, **oversee internal audit** (CAE appointment/removal/compensation, plan, budget, remediation timeliness). *(Force: guideline + rule · D3 cluster)*
6. Through the audit committee, **oversee the external audit and ICFR**, with the SOX 404(b) attestation deemed to satisfy the Part 363 ICFR assessment; designated-laws compliance as annex; aligned cycle. *(Floor: FDI Act §36 (satisfied, not deleted) · D4)*
7. Where trust powers exist, the audit committee **may satisfy the fiduciary-audit duty** where composition overlaps (12 CFR 9.9 pattern, generalized). *(Lever 1.10)*

**Capital & liquidity**
8. **Approve the capital plan, capital policy, and capital actions**; receive methodology reporting (scenario design, model limitations) as information, not approval items. *(Floor: 12 U.S.C. 5365(i) oversight · Force: regulation · D5)*
9. **Approve the liquidity risk tolerance** — one approval satisfying Reg YY and guidance. *(Force: regulation · lever 1.4)*

**Resilience**
10. Run **one annual resilience-planning cycle** covering recovery, contingency funding, and resolution content as modules; approve the shared resolution submission with the FDIC-interest IDI annex. *(Floor: 12 U.S.C. 5365(d) (submission survives; format shared) · R4)*

**Credit & counterparty**
11. **Approve the credit-risk governance framework** (one interagency real-estate-lending standard; ACL/credit-review folded into the risk-committee credit cycle). *(Lever 1.8)*
12. **Insider/affiliate duties retained as-is** — Reg O / 23A-23B review processes coordinated, not consolidated (parallel-distinct statutory purposes). *(Floors: 12 U.S.C. 371c/375b lineage · lever 1.3, non-target)*

**Operations & technology**
13. **Approve one enterprise information-security program** (replacing the four parallel appendix approvals); one operational incident-notification standard (value/timeline selection TBD). *(Floor-adjacent: GLBA §501(b) program survives · lever 1.12)*
14. **Third-party risk:** board attention only for relationships above the quantitative critical-activity threshold (G6 definition; value TBD); management approval plus aggregate reporting below it; CFPB service-provider concern retained. *(R6)*
15. Operational resilience/business continuity: **folded into the risk-appetite approval** (entry 1), not a separate board cycle. *(Lever 1.13)*
16. Model risk governance: framework approval via G1; no separate board-level restatement. *(B5 wave 2)*

**Financial crime & consumer**
17. **Approve the BSA/AML compliance program** — retained intact; exam manual cross-references the rule rather than restating it; the two pending rewrites reconciled before layering compounds. *(Floor: 31 U.S.C. 5318(h); 12 U.S.C. 1818(s) · non-target, coordinated)*
18. **Consumer compliance oversight retained** — coordinated CMS expectations, no consolidation (distinct statutory regime). *(Lever 1.16, non-target)*

**People & governance**
19. **Compensation:** the compensation committee oversees incentive-compensation arrangements; the §956 disposition (finalize / rely on guidance / repeal) is an open policy branch for principals — both branches decomposed and ready. *(R3)*
20. **CEO and senior-officer performance and succession** (CEO objectives and accountability; CRO/CAE succession per firm). *(SR 21-3 Attribute 3 lineage, restated once)*
21. **Director nomination, eligibility, and continuity** — one process, one interagency change-in-director notice procedure. *(Lever 1.9)*
22. **One consolidated director-training program**, with the BSA module deemed to satisfy the statutory training pillar (floor preserved via G3-style deem). *(R2)*
23. **Board structure:** leadership, executive-committee limits, committee charters — General-Part mechanics (G1/G4), stated once. *(B5 wave 6)*
24. **Hold management accountable** — the residual active-oversight duty (App. D III.B / rating-framework lineage): the board's irreducible function, deliberately *not* consolidated into anything. *(Retained verbatim)*

## What changes, and what does not

**Removed:** ~190 of the ~227 statements — as *restatements* (same duty said again in guidance/manuals: now cross-references), *mirror pairs* (parallel BHC/IDI approvals: now G3 reliance), and formal repeals of already-superseded remnants. **No substantive safeguard is deleted:** all six floor anchors survive; every parallel-but-justified interest (FDICIA vs. SOX, FDIC resolvability, Reg O, CFPB) survives by explicit relation; binding force is conserved everywhere except the declared SR 12-17→Reg YY codification.

**Open dials for principals (never set by the tool):** the critical-activity threshold value; the resilience-cycle cadence; the reconciled independence test's exact formulation; the §956 branch; the incident-notification timeline.

**Provenance:** every entry traces to source rows via `boarddistill/outputs/b5/corpus/master_crosswalk.md` and to its operations via the lever decompositions (`b0_decompositions.json`, `b5_residual_decompositions.json`, `outputs/b5/corpus/lever_decompositions.json`). Verification status per source row is in the crosswalk; 85 of 227 source rows remain `unverified` (guidance-manual strata), so entries resting mainly on those carry proportionally lower evidentiary weight.
